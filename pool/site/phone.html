A few kangaroos in your pocket

This page runs part of the search in your browser: real kangaroos on the real puzzle, walking the same graph as the GPUs. Every point it finds is checked, paid and searched exactly like a GPU's. A phone does about a million jumps a second against a 5090's nineteen billion, so this is a way to be part of it rather than a way to move the needle: expect a point every few hours, not every minute.
